Privacy Policy

App: Colorful
Developer: MeadowByte
Effective date: 2026-10-27

This policy explains how Colorful handles information when you use the app.

Information and photos

The app does not require an account. Coloring progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. If you choose a photo using the Android photo picker, the app copies that photo into its private app storage so it can create and save a coloring page. Photos and progress are not uploaded by the app to the developer or a server.

Storage and deletion

Imported photos and coloring progress remain in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete an imported photo in the app and reset coloring progress using the app's reset control. Uninstalling the app removes its private app data, subject to Android device backup and restore settings.

Sharing and third parties

The app does not sell or share your photos or coloring progress with the developer or third parties. This policy describes the app as currently distributed; if future versions add analytics, advertising, crash reporting, online services, or other SDKs that collect data, this policy will be updated to describe that data and its recipients.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The current version will be published at the policy URL shown on the app's store listing.
